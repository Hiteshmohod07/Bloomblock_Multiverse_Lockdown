EXACT GAME RULES

- Each valid move advances the player one orthogonal square.
- Walls, created barriers, and board boundaries block movement.
- Entering an uncollected fragment collects it and creates an energy barrier on the square the player LEFT.
- The fragment’s square remains walkable.
- Ordinary movement creates no barrier.
- Revisiting a collected fragment’s square creates no new barrier.
- Invalid moves change nothing, including move count and undo history.
- The portal is visually locked and inactive until every fragment is collected.
- A locked portal tile remains walkable; entering it early does not win.
- Victory occurs when the player is on the portal with every fragment collected.
- Block further movement after victory, while allowing undo and restart.
- Animation must not cause overlapping moves or change logical results.
