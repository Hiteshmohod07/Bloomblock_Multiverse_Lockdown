# BLOOMLOCK — LEVELS 3, 4 & 5 IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT
**For Person 1 (Driver / Developer)**

Implement Levels 3, 4, and 5 in the existing Bloomlock game. Preserve Levels 1 and 2, the current UI, and all existing mechanics. Do not rebuild the game from scratch.

The levels should increase in difficulty while remaining solvable under the existing rules.

## GLOBAL RULES — APPLY TO ALL THREE LEVELS

1. The game uses a tile-based grid with coordinates `(row, column)`, starting at `(0,0)` in the top-left.
2. When the player enters an uncollected fragment tile, the fragment is collected and the tile the player moved FROM becomes permanently sealed.
3. The fragment's destination tile remains walkable.
4. Ordinary movement creates no barriers.
5. Walls and sealed tiles block movement.
6. Collecting an already collected fragment must not create another barrier.
7. Time Rewind undoes the previous move and restores the exact previous state, including the player position, fragment collection, and barrier.
8. The exit remains walkable before all fragments are collected, but victory occurs only after every fragment is collected and the player reaches the exit.
9. Restart resets the player, fragments, barriers, and level state.
10. Preserve keyboard controls, touch controls, barrier preview, fragment counter, and victory detection.

# LEVEL 3 — THE FORBIDDEN ROUTE

**Difficulty:** Medium  
**Challenge:** Navigate around walls and collect fragments without blocking the route to the exit.

### Grid

```text
      0 1 2 3 4
Row 0 . . . . E
Row 1 # # . . .
Row 2 . . A . .
Row 3 . # # # .
Row 4 S . . B .
```

**Coordinates**
- Start: `(4,0)`
- Fragment A: `(2,2)`
- Fragment B: `(4,3)`
- Exit: `(0,4)`
- Walls: `(1,0)`, `(1,1)`, `(3,1)`, `(3,2)`, `(3,3)`

### Verified winning route

**RIGHT → RIGHT → RIGHT → RIGHT → UP → UP → LEFT → LEFT → UP → UP → RIGHT → RIGHT**

Expected walkthrough:
1. Move right four times, collecting Fragment B at `(4,3)` on the third move.
2. The tile `(4,2)` becomes sealed.
3. Move UP twice to `(2,4)`.
4. Move LEFT twice, collecting Fragment A at `(2,2)`.
5. The tile `(2,3)` becomes sealed.
6. Move UP twice to `(0,2)`.
7. Move RIGHT twice to the exit `(0,4)`.
8. Trigger victory.

**Expected barriers:** `(4,2)` and `(2,3)`.

# LEVEL 4 — THE FRACTURED TIMELINE

**Difficulty:** Medium–Hard  
**Challenge:** Plan a longer route through two fragments while learning to use Time Rewind strategically.

### Grid

```text
      0 1 2 3 4
Row 0 . . . . E
Row 1 . . . . .
Row 2 . . A . .
Row 3 . . . . .
Row 4 S . . . B
```

**Coordinates**
- Start: `(4,0)`
- Fragment A: `(2,2)`
- Fragment B: `(4,4)`
- Exit: `(0,4)`
- Walls: None

### Verified winning route

**UP → UP → RIGHT → RIGHT → DOWN → DOWN → RIGHT → RIGHT → UP → UP → UP → UP**

Expected walkthrough:
1. Move UP twice to `(2,0)`.
2. Move RIGHT twice, collecting Fragment A at `(2,2)`.
3. The tile `(2,1)` becomes sealed.
4. Move DOWN twice to `(4,2)`.
5. Move RIGHT twice, collecting Fragment B at `(4,4)`.
6. The tile `(4,3)` becomes sealed.
7. Move UP four times along column 4 to the exit `(0,4)`.
8. Trigger victory.

**Expected barriers:** `(2,1)` and `(4,3)`.

### Design intent
This level has fewer walls but a longer route. Use it to test the fragment counter, barrier previews, rewind behavior, and player understanding of the sealing rule.
